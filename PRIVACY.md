# Privacy Policy: SSS Team Research Skill

Last updated: 24 November 2026

SSS Team Research Skill ("the skill") is a set of instructions, reference documents and small local Python scripts. It runs inside the AI assistant you install it in (for example Claude, ChatGPT or Codex).

## What data the skill collects
None. The SSS Team does not run a server for this skill and does not receive, collect, store or sell any personal data. The skill contains no analytics, no tracking and no network calls.

## What happens to the content you share
When you share drafts, PDFs, data or prompts while using the skill, that content is processed by the AI platform you are using (Anthropic, OpenAI or another host), under that platform's own privacy policy and data settings. The SSS Team has no access to it.

## Local scripts
The bundled scripts (`prisma_drawio.py`, `selection_flow_drawio.py`, `double_stage_drawio.py`, `pipeline_drawio.py`, `paper_style.py`) read only the files or numbers you give them. They write diagram or chart files to the location you choose, run only inside your assistant's or your own environment, and never send data over the network.

## Children
The skill is intended for researchers and students aged 18 and over.

## Contact
For questions, open an issue at https://github.com/dayam8696/sss-team-research-skill/issues.
