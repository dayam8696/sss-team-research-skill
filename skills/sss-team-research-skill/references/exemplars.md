# Source corpus

The SSS house style in the type files was distilled from 28 papers by SSS team members and collaborators: 15 empirical papers, 6 reviews, 5 bibliometric analyses, 1 position paper and 1 supplement. The skill contains only paraphrased patterns, counts and templates. It contains no text, figures or PDFs from these papers. Papers that were under double-blind review or not yet public when the skill was built are counted but not listed.

Read the published papers below to see the house style in full.
